Five phases, one launch day

Dates are targets. If Google's approval moves, every later phase shifts by the same amount.

Phase 0 · FoundationOct 5 – Oct 18

Set the stage

  • Claim @forgelinksms everywhere; set up profiles with the icon and feature graphic.
  • Publish the product page and privacy policy on azureforgeai.com.
  • Build a demo phone with made-up contacts and record the clip library.
  • Upload the Google Play SMS-declaration video to YouTube (unlisted); open the closed-test track.
Phase 1 · TestersOct 19 – Nov 8

Recruit 25 testers and build in public

  • Sources in order: friends and family → Facebook groups → Reddit tester exchanges → LinkedIn → X.
  • Closed test runs Oct 26 – Nov 8; remind testers on days 1, 7 and 12 to keep the app installed.
  • Post 3–4 times a week: glow-up clips, one feature at a time, developer updates.
Phase 2 · Pre-launchNov 9 – Nov 16

Apply for production and tease the date

  • Submit the listing, Data safety answers and SMS permission declaration.
  • Countdown posts and Stories; tester quotes with permission.
  • Schedule the Product Hunt page and the YouTube walkthrough Premiere.
Phase 3 · LaunchTue Nov 17 – Sun Nov 22

Launch everywhere on one day

  • 12:01 a.m. Pacific: Product Hunt goes live; post the maker's first comment.
  • Morning: X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Premiere. Reddit post Wednesday morning.
  • Reply to every comment within two hours; one feature clip per day through Sunday.
  • Boost the best launch Reel on Facebook/Instagram.
Phase 4 · HolidaysNov 23 – Jan 10

Ride the season

  • Thanksgiving (Nov 26): family group chat survival — mute, snooze, scheduled greetings.
  • Black Friday / Cyber Monday: "Every store is about to text you — Updates catches them."
  • December: holiday greetings, forwarding photos to grandparents, Screener vs holiday spam.
  • New Year: "Inbox reset for 2027" with Trash, Archive and filters.

Protect the account

Rules for every platform

  • Follow each community's self-promotion rules. Reddit and Facebook groups ban accounts that only post links — participate first.
  • Never promise what isn't built. No encryption, RCS or texting from a computer yet; say "on the roadmap" only if it truly is.
  • Show the differences, don't trash competitors. Link to the fair comparison on the product page.
  • Ask before quoting a tester or showing their name.
  • Keep the story true. The ready-to-post copy uses personal details as a starting point — edit each one so it's accurate before posting.
  • Stay on brand: the ForgeLink icon, navy/blue/amber colors, and one pitch — "Your people up front. Everything else sorted."
