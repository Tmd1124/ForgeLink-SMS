# ForgeLink SMS — Master Promotion Plan

**Goal:** get ForgeLink SMS through Google Play's closed-test requirement, launch it publicly with momentum, and grow steady installs through the holiday season.

**One-line pitch (use everywhere):** *ForgeLink SMS — your people up front, everything else sorted.*

**Short description (80 characters, matches the Play listing):** Fast, private texting with smart lanes, scheduled sends, and a spam screener.

## 3. Content pillars

Every post belongs to one of these, so the feed stays varied:

1. **Glow-up** — before/after of a cluttered inbox. The hook that stops the scroll.
2. **Feature in 15 seconds** — one feature, one clip, one sentence.
3. **Real life** — sports parents, family group chats, holidays, "Mom texted 4 times".
4. **Private by design** — no ads, no trackers, no account, your messages stay on your phone.
5. **Build in public** — the developer's story, decisions, bugs fixed, what's next (strongest on LinkedIn, X, Reddit).
6. **Community** — tester shout-outs, feature requests, polls ("Bubbles or color list?").

## 4. Timeline

Dates are targets. If Google's approval moves, shift every later phase by the same amount.

### Phase 0 — Foundation · Oct 5 – Oct 18, 2026
- Claim handles, set up profiles with the icon and feature graphic.
- Publish the product page and privacy policy.
- Build demo data and record the clip library (Section 2).
- Upload the 2-minute walkthrough to YouTube (unlisted first — it doubles as the Play Store SMS-declaration video).
- Open the Play Console closed-test track.

### Phase 1 — Recruit testers + build in public · Oct 19 – Nov 8
- **Target 25 testers** (Google needs 12 active for 14 straight days; the buffer covers drop-outs).
- Sources, in order: friends and family → Facebook groups you're already in (teams, school, church, neighborhood) → Reddit tester communities → LinkedIn network → X.
- Closed test runs **Oct 26 – Nov 8** (14 days minimum). Remind testers on day 1, 7 and 12 to keep the app installed.
- Post 3–4 times a week everywhere: glow-up, feature clips, build-in-public updates.

### Phase 2 — Pre-launch · Nov 9 – Nov 16
- Apply for production access; submit the listing, Data safety and SMS declaration.
- Teasers: "Launching next week", countdown Stories, tester quotes (with permission).
- Line up Product Hunt: hunter/maker profile, gallery images, first comment written.
- Email everyone on the tester list the launch date.

### Phase 3 — Launch week · Tue Nov 17 – Sun Nov 22
- **Tuesday Nov 17, 12:01 a.m. Pacific:** Product Hunt launch.
- Same morning: posts on every platform, Reddit launch post, LinkedIn story, X thread, YouTube premiere of the walkthrough.
- Reply to every comment within the first 2 hours on each platform.
- Wed–Sun: one feature clip per day; share early reviews.

### Phase 4 — Holiday growth · Nov 23, 2026 – Jan 10, 2027
- **Thanksgiving (Nov 26):** "Family group chat survival" — bubbles, mute, snooze, scheduled "Happy Thanksgiving".
- **Black Friday / Cyber Monday (Nov 27 – Nov 30):** "Every store is about to text you — ForgeLink files them in Updates."
- **December:** scheduled holiday messages, forwarding photos to grandparents, Screener vs holiday spam.
- **New Year:** "Inbox reset for 2027" — clean-up with Trash, Archive and filters.
- Weekly: one tip, one real-life post, one community post per platform.

## 8. Rules that protect the account

- Follow each community's self-promotion rules — Reddit and Facebook groups ban accounts that only post links. Participate first.
- Never promise features that don't exist yet (encryption, RCS, texting from a computer). Say "on the roadmap" only if it truly is.
- Don't claim you're "better than Google Messages" in ads; show the differences and let people decide. The comparison on the product page is accurate and fair — link to it.
- Ask before quoting a tester or showing their name.
- The ready-to-post copy uses personal details as a starting point ("my kids' texts", softball, 30 years building systems). Edit every story so it's true to you before posting.
- Keep the brand consistent: the ForgeLink icon, the navy/blue/amber colors, the one-line pitch.
