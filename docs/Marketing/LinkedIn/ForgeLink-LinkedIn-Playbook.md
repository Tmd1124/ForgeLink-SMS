# ForgeLink SMS — LinkedIn Playbook

**Role:** credibility for AzureForge AI and the founder. ForgeLink is proof that AzureForge ships real, polished products — valuable for your consulting clients as much as for app installs. LinkedIn also reaches testers among your professional network.
**Audience:** your network (engineering leaders, CTOs, clients, peers), Android and .NET professionals.

## Set up (Phase 0)

- Post from **your personal profile** (founder posts reach far more people than company pages).
- Add ForgeLink SMS as a **Project** on your profile (Featured section: product page link + feature graphic).
- On the **AzureForge AI company page**, add a "Products" mention and reshare your posts.
- Headline tweak for launch month: `… | Building ForgeLink SMS`.

## How to publish

- **Text posts:** 150–300 words, short paragraphs, a strong first line (it's all people see before "…more").
- **Links:** put the link in the **first comment**, not the post body — posts with outside links tend to get less reach.
- **Media:** a native video (16:9 or 1:1, under 90 s) or a 4–6 page PDF carousel (1080×1350) works best.
- **Hashtags:** 3 at the end: `#Android #dotnet #ProductDevelopment` (vary: `#BuildInPublic #Privacy #MobileApps`).

## When to publish

- **Tue–Thu, 8–10 a.m. Eastern.**
- **Cadence:** 1–2 posts a week. Comment on 3–5 posts from your network the same morning.

### Tue Oct 27 — engineering lesson (PDF carousel)
**"5 things I learned building an Android SMS app in 2026"**
1. Becoming the default SMS app is a role, and Google Play only allows SMS permissions after you hold it.
2. Android 16 enforces edge-to-edge and predictive back — both changed how my app handled screens and the back button.
3. Target API 36 meant upgrading to .NET 10 mid-project.
4. Privacy is an architecture choice: no servers means no data to leak.
5. Test on real hardware — a foldable's two screens caught bugs an emulator never would.
