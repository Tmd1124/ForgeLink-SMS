# ForgeLink SMS — Facebook Playbook

**Role:** tester recruitment, community, and the launch-week paid boost.
**Audience:** parents, grandparents, sports-team families, church and neighborhood groups — people drowning in group texts, school alerts and store coupons.

## Set up (Phase 0)

- **Page name:** ForgeLink SMS · **Username:** @forgelinksms · **Category:** App Page / Software
- **Profile picture:** `assets/forgelink-icon.png` · **Cover:** `assets/forgelink-feature.png`
- **Intro:** Your people up front. Everything else sorted. A private texting app for Android — no ads, no trackers, no account.
- **Button:** "Sign Up" → tester sign-up form (switch to "Install App" at launch).
- **Links:** product page, privacy policy, email Tmd1124@outlook.com.
- Link the Page to your Instagram account so Reels cross-post.

## How to publish

| Format | Spec | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Reel | 9:16, 1080×1920, 15–30 s, captions on | Glow-up and feature clips (same files as TikTok/Instagram) |
| Photo post | 1080×1350 (4:5) | Tips, before/after images, polls |
| Text post in groups | Plain text + one image, no link in the first line | Tester recruitment and real-life stories |
| Event | "ForgeLink launch day" | Launch countdown |

- Post from the **Page**, then share into groups as yourself **only where the group allows promotion**.
- Put links in the **first comment** rather than the post when posting in groups — many groups auto-hide link posts.
- Turn on captions for every video; most Facebook video is watched muted.

## When to publish

- **Best times to start with:** Tue–Thu, 9–11 a.m. and 7–9 p.m. Eastern. Check Page Insights after 3 weeks and move to your audience's peak hours.
- **Cadence:** Phase 1 three posts/week; launch week daily; afterward 3–4/week.

## Groups strategy

1. Start with groups you already belong to (teams, school, church, neighborhood, alumni). Read each group's rules; ask an admin before posting.
2. Share a story, not an ad: "I got tired of missing my kid's texts under store coupons, so I built this."
3. Join 2–3 Android tester groups (search "Android app testers" or "Google Play closed testing") for tester exchanges — test others' apps in return.
4. Consider starting a **"ForgeLink SMS Testers & Tips"** group after launch for feedback and feature requests.

## Measure

Reach, link clicks, tester sign-ups, and installs from the boost (use a separate UTM link per post). Keep what earns sign-ups; drop what doesn't.
