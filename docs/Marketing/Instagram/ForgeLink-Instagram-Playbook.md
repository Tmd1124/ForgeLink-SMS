# ForgeLink SMS — Instagram Playbook

**Role:** the visual showcase. Instagram rewards exactly what makes ForgeLink different: floating bubbles, color, and a calm-looking inbox.
**Audience:** 20–45 Android users who care how their phone looks; parents; small-business owners who text customers.

## Set up (Phase 0)

- **Handle:** @forgelinksms · **Account type:** Professional → Creator or Business (category: App Page).
- **Name field:** ForgeLink SMS | Private texting (the name field is searchable).
- **Bio:**
  > 🫧 Your people up front. Everything else sorted.
  > 📦 Smart lanes · 🛡️ Spam screener · 🔒 No ads, no trackers
  > 👇 Join the Android test
- **Link:** product page (or a link page with Join the test / Watch the demo).
- **Highlights:** "Features", "How-to", "Testers", "Updates" — covers in the brand navy with a single emoji.
- Connect to the Facebook Page for cross-posting.

## How to publish

| Format | Spec | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Reel | 1080×1920 (9:16), 7–30 s, text inside the center safe area | Main growth format — glow-up and feature clips |
| Carousel | 1080×1350 (4:5), 5–8 slides | "5 things your texting app should do", comparison, how-tos |
| Story | 1080×1920, polls/countdown stickers | Daily behind-the-scenes, launch countdown |

**Reel formula:** hook text in the first second ("My texts were chaos"), the screen recording, one caption line, a trending but calm audio track at low volume. Keep on-screen text large and inside the middle 4:5 area so the grid crop doesn't cut it.

**Caption formula:** one-line hook → 2–3 short lines → call to action → 5–8 hashtags.

## When to publish

- Start with **Mon–Fri 11 a.m.–1 p.m. and 7–9 p.m. Eastern**; adjust from Insights after a month.
- **Cadence:** 3 Reels + 1 carousel a week; Stories most days (they keep you in followers' feeds).
