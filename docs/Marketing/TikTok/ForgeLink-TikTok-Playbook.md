# ForgeLink SMS — TikTok Playbook

**Role:** the widest organic reach for a brand-new account. TikTok shows videos to people who don't follow you yet, so a single good clip can reach thousands.
**Audience:** 18–45, "app finds" and "tech tips" viewers, parents, people who complain about spam texts.

## Set up (Phase 0)

- **Handle:** @forgelinksms · **Account:** Business account (unlocks analytics and a bio link once eligible).
- **Bio (80 characters):** `Your people up front. Everything else sorted. 📱 Android texting app`
- **Profile picture:** the ForgeLink icon.

## How to publish

- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, **7–25 seconds**. Shorter videos get re-watched, which TikTok rewards.
- **Hook in the first second** — on-screen text plus your voice or a text-to-speech line.
- **Show the phone screen full-size**; no slow intros, no logo animation.
- **Captions on** (TikTok's auto-captions) and a short caption with 3–5 hashtags.
- Use a **trending sound at low volume** under a voice-over, or TikTok's text-to-speech.
- Post natively (upload in the TikTok app); don't post videos with another app's watermark.
- Reply to comments **with a video** — those replies often outperform the original.

## When to publish

- Start with **Tue–Thu, 7–9 a.m. and 6–9 p.m. Eastern**; switch to your own peak hours from Analytics after 3 weeks.
- **Cadence:** 4–5 videos a week in Phases 1–3 (consistency matters more than polish here), 3 a week after.

## Do and don't

- **Do** post consistently, reply to comments, and repeat what works (a winning format can be re-shot 3–4 times).
- **Don't** show real conversations, delete underperforming videos (it can hurt reach), or buy followers.

## Measure

Average watch time and completion rate first, then shares, profile visits and link taps.
