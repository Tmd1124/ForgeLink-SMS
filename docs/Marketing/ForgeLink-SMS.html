Why people switch

The ForgeLink Advantage

Every messaging app treats your inbox as one long list. That list is where important messages go to get lost.

The typical hurdle

Family buried under business texts

Your daughter's text sits between a delivery notice, a bank alert and a store coupon. By the time you scroll to it, it's an hour old.

The fix: Your favorites and unread people float at the top as bubbles, and business texts move to an Updates lane grouped by topic.
The typical hurdle

Strangers interrupt your day

Wrong numbers, spam and sales pitches buzz your phone exactly like the people you actually know.

The fix: Unknown numbers arrive silently in the Screener. One tap Allows them into Chats — or Blocks them for good.
The typical hurdle

Your messages feed someone's business

Many free apps are paid for with your data: accounts, cloud copies, analytics and ads.

The fix: No ads, no trackers, no accounts. ForgeLink has no servers — your messages never leave your phone except when you send them.
Capabilities matrix

Everything a texting app should do — and a lot nobody else does

ForgeLink is a complete replacement for your phone's messaging app: texts, picture messages, group chats and more, plus the tools that make a busy inbox calm.

01

Bubbles & Displays

Make the main screen yours. Three layouts and presets you can fine-tune.

  • Bubbles, Bubbles + color list, or Color list
  • You choose who's a bubble: favorites, unread, recent, filtered or everyone
  • Color by person, by filter, or not at all
  • Pick the unread color — or none
02

Smart Lanes

Chats, Updates and Screener keep people, businesses and strangers apart automatically.

  • Updates grouped by topic: codes, banking, deliveries, shopping
  • Updates and Screener arrive silently
  • Verification codes still notify — with one-tap Copy
  • Allow or Block unknown senders in one tap
03

Color Filters

Build your own groups — Family, Team, Work — with a color and an emoji.

  • Filter tabs pinned under the lanes, swipe to scroll
  • Pick a filter, search, and tap to add anyone to it
  • Rows can take their filter's color
  • Emoji picker built into filter names
04

Messaging Power Tools

Everything you expect, plus the shortcuts you'll wonder how you lived without.

  • Picture, video and real group messages (MMS)
  • Voice messages, reactions, reply-with-quote
  • Quick replies and schedule-send — even to groups
  • Forward with a "Forwarded to…" record
05

Stay in Control

Handle your messages on your terms — and undo when you change your mind.

  • Reply or mark read right from the notification
  • Mute for an hour, 8 hours, a day or always
  • Snooze a conversation until you're ready
  • Archive, block, and Trash with 30-day recovery
06

Find Anything

Your history is only useful if you can get back to it quickly.

  • Search every conversation, or inside one
  • Photos & links gallery for each conversation
  • Drafts saved automatically everywhere
  • Swipe right or press Back to return to your list

Good to know — where the others still lead

  • Chat features over data (RCS / iMessage): Google Messages and iMessage add typing indicators, full-resolution media and end-to-end encryption when both people use them. ForgeLink uses standard SMS/MMS, which works with every phone but isn't end-to-end encrypted.
  • Texting from a computer: Google Messages for web and Messages on Mac let you text from a computer. ForgeLink is phone-only today.

Private by design

Your messages are none of our business.

ForgeLink stores everything in your phone's own message storage and the app's private space. There are no ForgeLink servers, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. Messages leave your phone only when you send them — through your carrier, like any text.

Link previews are only fetched for links from your contacts, your groups and yourself, so a stranger can't learn you opened their message.
