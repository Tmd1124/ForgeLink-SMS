# ForgeLink SMS — Product Hunt Playbook

**Role:** one concentrated launch day that reaches early adopters and tech writers, and leaves a lasting page (and possibly a "Product of the Day" badge) you can link from everywhere.
**Launch date:** **Tuesday, November 17, 2026** — the day launches go live at 12:01 a.m. Pacific (3:01 a.m. Eastern) and run for 24 hours.

## Set up (Phase 0 – Phase 2)

- Create your **maker profile** now and use it to upvote and comment on other launches for a few weeks — a brand-new account with no activity carries less weight.
- **Product page fields:**
  - **Name:** ForgeLink SMS
  - **Tagline (60 characters max):** `Your people up front. Everything else sorted.`
  - **Description (260 characters max):**
    > A private texting app for Android. Favorites float at the top as bubbles, bank and delivery texts sort into Updates by topic, and unknown numbers wait silently in a Screener. Color filters, scheduled texts, quick replies. No ads, no trackers, no account.
  - **Topics:** Android, Messaging, Privacy, Productivity
  - **Links:** Google Play listing, product page
  - **Pricing:** Paid (or Free, matching the Play listing)
- **Media:**
  - **Thumbnail:** 240×240 — the ForgeLink icon (an animated GIF of a bubble glowing works well).
  - **Gallery:** 5–6 images at 1270×760: (1) feature graphic with the tagline, (2) Bubbles, (3) Updates lane, (4) Screener, (5) Display settings with the three looks, (6) privacy — "No ads · No trackers · No account".
  - **Video:** the YouTube walkthrough.
- Schedule the launch in advance so the page goes live automatically at 12:01 a.m. PT.

## Maker's first comment (post it the moment the page goes live)

> Hi Product Hunt 👋 I'm the maker of ForgeLink SMS.
>
> My texts were one long list — my family's messages sat between bank alerts, delivery notices and spam. Every messaging app treats them all the same, so I built one that doesn't:
>
> 🫧 **Bubbles** — favorites and unread people float at the top (three layouts to choose from)
> 📦 **Updates** — business texts grouped by topic, without notifications (verification codes still notify)
> 🛡️ **Screener** — unknown numbers wait quietly until you Allow or Block
> 🏷️ **Color filters** with emoji, pinned as tabs
> 🔒 **Private by design** — no ads, no analytics, no account, no servers
>
> Honest limits: it's SMS/MMS on Android (no RCS or iPhone) and phone-only for now.
>
> I'd love your feedback — especially on the home-screen designs. I'll be here all day answering questions!

## Do and don't

- **Do** ask people to "check it out and share feedback on Product Hunt".
- **Don't** ask for upvotes directly, trade upvotes, or have people create new accounts just to upvote — Product Hunt detects it and can penalize the launch.
- **Do** answer tough questions openly (encryption, iPhone, price).
- **Do** add the Product Hunt badge to the product page after launch.

## Measure

Upvotes, comments, daily rank, and visits to the Google Play listing from Product Hunt (use a tagged link).
