# ForgeLink SMS — YouTube Playbook

**Role:** search and proof. People search YouTube for "best texting app for Android" and "how to stop spam texts" — a good video keeps getting found for years. YouTube also hosts the **demo video Google requires** for the SMS permission declaration.
**Audience:** Android users actively looking for a better messaging app; testers who want to see it before installing.

## Set up (Phase 0)

- **Channel name:** ForgeLink SMS · **Handle:** @forgelinksms
- **Banner:** 2560×1440 built from the feature graphic (keep text inside the center 1546×423 safe area).
- **Description:** ForgeLink SMS is a private texting app for Android that puts your people up front and sorts everything else. No ads, no trackers, no account. Contact: Tmd1124@outlook.com
- **Links:** product page, privacy policy, Google Play (at launch).
- **Playlists:** "Getting started", "Features in 30 seconds", "Build log".

## How to publish

| Format | Spec | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Short | 9:16, up to 60 s | The same clips as TikTok/Reels, re-uploaded natively |
| Walkthrough | 16:9, 1920×1080, 2–4 min | Main demo, Play Store promo video, SMS-declaration video |
| Build log | 16:9, 5–8 min, monthly | Developer story, what shipped, what's next |

**Every long video:**
- **Title** leads with what people search: "Best Texting App for Android? ForgeLink SMS Walkthrough".
- **Thumbnail:** phone screen with the bubbles + 3–4 big words ("Texts, Sorted.") on navy, amber accent.
- **Description:** first two lines say what it is and link to Google Play; then chapters (timestamps), links, and hashtags `#android #textmessages #androidapps`.
- **Captions:** upload or correct the auto-captions.
- **End screen:** subscribe + next video.

## When to publish

- Shorts: 3 a week (the same days as TikTok, a few hours later).
- Long videos: **Saturday or Sunday morning** ET works well for how-to viewing; 1 in Phase 0, 1 at launch, then 1–2 a month.
- Premiere the launch walkthrough (scheduled Premiere lets you share the link in advance).

## Videos to make

### 1. Google Play SMS-declaration video (Phase 0 — unlisted)
30–60 seconds, screen recording, no music:
1. Fresh install → open ForgeLink SMS → setup screen.
2. Tap "1. Make this my default SMS app" → accept the Android prompt.
3. Tap "2. Grant permissions" (it only unlocks now) → allow → Continue.
4. Conversation list → open a conversation → send a text → receive a reply.
Title: "ForgeLink SMS — default SMS app setup (Google Play review)". Keep it **unlisted**; paste the link into the Play Console declaration form.
