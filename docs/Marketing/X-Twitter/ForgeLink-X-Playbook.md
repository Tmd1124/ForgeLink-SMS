# ForgeLink SMS — X (Twitter) Playbook

**Role:** build in public, connect with Android developers and tech writers, and amplify launch day. X is where indie developers and journalists notice new apps.
**Audience:** Android developers, .NET/MAUI community, indie makers, tech press.

## Set up (Phase 0)

- **Handle:** @forgelinksms (also post from your personal account — people follow people).
- **Bio:** `Your people up front. Everything else sorted. A private texting app for Android. No ads, no trackers. Built by @AzureForgeAI` (swap in your real company handle).
- **Header:** the feature graphic. **Pinned post:** the tester call, then the launch post.

## How to publish

- **Posts:** under 280 characters, one idea each, one image or short video.
- **Threads:** 5–8 posts for stories (why I built it, how a feature works).
- **Video:** the same vertical clips, or 16:9 screen recordings under 60 seconds.
- **Tags that help:** `#AndroidDev #buildinpublic #indiedev #dotnet #MAUI` — 1–2 per post, never more.
- Tag relevant communities or accounts only when genuinely relevant (e.g. .NET MAUI when you talk about building it with MAUI).

## When to publish

- **Weekdays, 8–10 a.m. and 12–1 p.m. Eastern.**
- **Cadence:** 1 post a day on weekdays in Phases 1–3; 3–4 a week after. Reply to others daily — replies grow a new account faster than posts.

**Wed Oct 21 — thread: "How ForgeLink sorts your texts"**
1. Every texting app shows one long list. ForgeLink splits it into three lanes. 🧵
2. Chats: people you know, anyone you've replied to or allowed.
3. Updates: short codes, toll-free and named senders — banks, deliveries, stores — grouped by topic. No notifications, except verification codes.
4. Screener: unknown numbers you've never replied to. Silent until you tap Allow.
5. On top of that: your favorites and unread people float as bubbles.
6. All of it runs on your phone. No servers, no account, no ads.
7. Testers wanted → [link]
